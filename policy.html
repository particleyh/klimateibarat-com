At عبارات وكلمات - اقتباسات و حكم , we are committed to protecting your privacy and respecting your data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when using our app.

Our app uses Firebase and AdMob services for analytics and to collect information about app performance and crash reports. This may include non-personal information such as device type, operating system version, locale settings, and browser type.

We use the collected information to improve app performance and user experience, as well as to analyze and resolve app crashes. The data gathered through Firebase and AdMob helps us understand app usage and performance. We do not use the information to identify users personally.

The app may request limited permissions to access Firebase and AdMob services for performance analysis, but it does not request access to any personal data on your device.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with any third parties. All data for crash reports and analytics via Firebase and AdMob is collected anonymously and is subject to the privacy policies of these services.

5. Security

We are committed to protecting your data and taking appropriate measures to ensure data security. The information collected by Firebase and AdMob is stored in a secure environment.

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here, along with an updated effective date at the top. We recommend reviewing this page periodically to stay informed about our latest privacy practices.
